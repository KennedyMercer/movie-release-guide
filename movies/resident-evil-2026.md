## What Is Resident Evil (2026) About?

The story centers on **Bryan**, a medical courier who is sent to deliver a package to a remote hospital.

What begins as a seemingly ordinary job quickly turns into a dangerous night of survival as an outbreak spreads around him and mutated creatures begin to appear.

Rather than directly retelling one of the earlier Resident Evil movies, the film introduces a new story set within the survival-horror world associated with the franchise.

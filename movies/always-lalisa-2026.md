## What Is Always Lalisa About?

**Always Lalisa** follows LISA during a pivotal year as she begins defining her identity and career outside the group that made her a global star.

The documentary captures her work on the solo album *Alter Ego*, her acting debut in the third season of *The White Lotus*, and preparations for her first major solo performance at the 2025 Coachella Music Festival.

It also explores the pressures and uncertainty that come with building a new path while remaining one of the world's most recognizable K-pop performers.

## LISA's Solo Journey

A central part of **Always Lalisa** is LISA's transition into a more independent phase of her career.

The documentary follows her as she writes, records and releases *Alter Ego*, develops her identity as a solo performer and takes on new opportunities outside music.

Her acting debut in *The White Lotus* and preparations for Coachella are also part of the story.

---

## From BLACKPINK to a New Chapter

LISA left Thailand as a teenager to train in South Korea before eventually debuting as a member of **BLACKPINK**.

After years of performing as part of one of the world's biggest K-pop groups, the documentary captures a period in which she is able to explore her own creative direction more independently.

That transition becomes one of the main themes of **Always Lalisa**.

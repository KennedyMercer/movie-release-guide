## What Is Street Fighter (2026) About?

Set in 1993, the movie follows **Ryu and Ken Masters**, two estranged fighters who are drawn back into combat when **Chun-Li** recruits them for the World Warrior Tournament.

What initially appears to be a martial-arts competition develops into something more dangerous as the fighters uncover a conspiracy surrounding the tournament.

The movie brings characters from Capcom's long-running fighting-game franchise into a new live-action theatrical adaptation.

## The Story Behind Street Fighter

The new film centers on Ryu and Ken, whose paths cross again after Chun-Li brings them into the World Warrior Tournament.

The competition places a large group of fighters together, but events surrounding the tournament point toward a wider conspiracy.

The story is set in **1993**, giving the adaptation a period setting connected to the era when Street Fighter was becoming a major arcade phenomenon.

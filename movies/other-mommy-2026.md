## What Is Other Mommy About?

**Other Mommy** follows **Bela**, an eight-year-old girl living with her family when she begins encountering a mysterious presence inside their home.

The entity appears and sounds almost exactly like Bela's mother.

As Bela develops a relationship with the strange figure, its intentions become increasingly disturbing and the threat to her family begins to grow.

The story combines supernatural horror with the unsettling idea that something familiar and trusted may not be what it appears to be.

## The Story Behind Other Mommy

The movie is adapted from **Josh Malerman's 2024 novel _Incidents Around the House_**.

The story is largely experienced through the perspective of young Bela as an entity begins appearing inside her home.

Rather than presenting itself as an obvious monster, the presence takes on the familiar appearance of Bela's mother.

That similarity becomes one of the central sources of tension as Bela struggles to understand what the entity wants and whether she can trust it.

---

## Jessica Chastain's Dual Role

One of the movie's central ideas is **Jessica Chastain playing two connected but very different figures**.

She appears as Bela's real mother while also portraying the manifestation known as **Other Mommy**.

The supernatural entity resembles Bela's mother closely enough to create uncertainty and fear inside the family home.

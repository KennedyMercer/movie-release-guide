## What Is Digger About?

**Digger** centers on Digger Rockwell, a powerful businessman who finds himself facing the consequences of a global catastrophe connected to his own actions.

As the crisis grows, Digger launches a frantic effort to prove that he can become humanity's savior before the disaster spirals completely out of control.

The movie combines black comedy, satire and contemporary themes within an original story created for the screen.

## The Story Behind Digger

At the center of the movie is Digger Rockwell, an enormously powerful figure whose actions contribute to a potentially devastating global crisis.

Rather than retreating as the situation worsens, Digger attempts to position himself as the person capable of fixing the catastrophe.

The story uses that conflict as the basis for a satirical examination of power, responsibility and self-image.
